### Description
Banner's display prominent messages and related optional actions. Variants include Banners for Alerts and Informative messages. Banners are site wide messages that are positioned at the very top of the screen.

### When to use it
Use Banners to communicate critical information and Alerts that may affect a user's experience across the site. Example: A planned outage or an upcoming feature

### When not to use it
Do not use a Banner to communicate Errors or minor messages.
