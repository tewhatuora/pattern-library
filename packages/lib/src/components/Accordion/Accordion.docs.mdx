### Description
Accordions save vertical space on a page by showing and hiding multiple blocks of content.

### When to use it
Use Accordions to allow users to progressively disclose information that’s relevant to them.

Use Accordions to display an overview of multiple, related sections of content. Accordions allow users to look across information that might otherwise be on different pages, and choose to show and hide sections that are relevant to them.

### When not to use it
Accordions work best for simple content and links. Do not use accordions to split up a series of questions. Use separate pages instead.

Do not put accordions within accordions, as it will make content difficult to find.

Do not use the accordion component if the amount of content inside will make the page slow to load.
